The details

Is Forme an operating salon?

No. Forme is a fictional Coastal Web Works design example. It demonstrates a website direction for a salon, barber or beauty studio.

Can appointment booking be added?

Booking options can be discussed as part of your project. This demo does not include a live booking system.

Can the brand and menu be changed?

Yes. Your real brand, photography and services would shape the final website, with scope and price agreed before work begins.
