Before we get started

A few good questions.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on the pages, features, content, and support you need. We’ll agree on the scope and quote before starting.

Can I use my existing domain?

Yes. We can connect your existing domain when the new website is ready, while keeping the registration in your name.

Can I request changes after launch?

Yes. Individual updates or ongoing care can be arranged. The scope and cost are agreed in advance.

Will the website work on phones?

Responsive layouts are part of the design, with attention to readable text, usable navigation, and clear contact options.
