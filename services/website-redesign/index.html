Start with what is getting in the way

A redesign starts with your current website and what you want it to do better. That might be outdated services, hard-to-read mobile pages, missing project photos, confusing navigation, or a contact process customers struggle to find.

Keep the useful parts of your existing site

We identify the content, branding, and domain you want to keep, then agree on the changes. A refresh can involve reorganizing information, updating photos, improving page layouts, and simplifying contact options. The scope and price are agreed before work begins.

Plan the move before launch

If existing pages or URLs are changing, those changes need a plan. We review important links and contact routes as part of the agreed launch work. Domain registration can remain in your name when the refreshed website is connected.

Review the result before going live

You review the website and share feedback before launch. Clear service descriptions, real examples of your work, and usable mobile layouts are more useful than adding pages that repeat keywords without helping customers.
