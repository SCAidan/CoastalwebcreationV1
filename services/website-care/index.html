Updates as your business changes

New services, different opening hours, recent work, and updated contact details all affect what customers need from your website. Coastal Web Works offers individual updates and ongoing care, with the included work and cost agreed before starting.

A clear way to request a change

Clients with portal access can submit website change requests and review the response there. Describe what needs changing and which page it belongs on. If an update needs more information or goes beyond the agreed scope, we discuss that before the work begins.

Hosting and domain responsibilities stay clear

Website care can include coordinating hosting and domain details according to your agreement. Who owns the accounts, who pays for renewals, and what support is included should be recorded so there are no surprises later.

Check the information customers actually use

Useful maintenance starts with accurate services, photos, hours, and contact information. Analytics, form checks, and other monitoring need their own connected tools and agreed scope; they are not implied by a website care request.
