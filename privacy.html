Privacy

This review version prepares inquiries in your own email application. The form does not transmit or store submissions on a website server. Information is sent only if you send the prepared email.

Google Fonts may receive your IP address when fonts load. Hosting providers may process technical request logs. No analytics or advertising tracking has been added to this version.

The separate dashboard defaults to a browser-local preview. If the owner configures and signs into Supabase, dashboard records are stored in that owner’s cloud workspace. The optional AI connection sends selected saved business and financial fields to OpenAI to answer the owner’s questions. It is disabled by default. Emails, phone numbers and free-form notes are omitted from AI requests. Review the setup documentation and provider policies before enabling these connections.
